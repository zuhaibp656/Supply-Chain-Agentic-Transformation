A Supply Chain agentic architecture is flexible, deterministic, and governed end to end.

Agents connect to SAP S/4HANA, Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), and Transportation Management Systems (TMS) where data already lives — in Google Cloud, on-premises at the plant, or across clouds. Identity, policy, guardrails and audit cover every step. Click any component, switch where it runs, or trace a live request.

Governance on every layer

  • Identity per agent — IAM agent identity, least privilege per plant & region
  • Agent registry — all 60 agents listed, owned, and switchable off
  • Prompt & response safety — Model Armor + DLP
  • Perimeter & keys — VPC Service Controls, customer-managed keys
  • Data governance — Dataplex catalogue, lineage, Cortex v7 quality
  • ✓Human approval — a named planner approves before any PO, STO, or tender executes
  • Audit & trace — Cloud Audit Logs, OpenTelemetry trace
